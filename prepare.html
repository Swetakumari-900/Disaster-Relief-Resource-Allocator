Disaster Preparedness

Learn essential tips to stay ready before an emergency strikes.

🏠 Home Safety ▼
  • Secure heavy furniture and wall hangings.
  • Install smoke detectors and check them monthly.
  • Know how to shut off water, gas, and electricity.
🧰 Emergency Kit ▼
  • Include water, non-perishable food, flashlight, batteries, and a first aid kit.
  • Pack necessary medications and copies of documents.
  • Store kit in an easily accessible place for all family members.
📞 Communication Plan ▼
  • Establish an out-of-area emergency contact.
  • Ensure every family member knows the plan.
  • Use text messaging if phone lines are busy.
